# Collateral

## Definition
**Collateral** in the perp-DEX context is the set of assets a trader posts to a venue account (or to a single position) that secure open-position losses, valued continuously at mark/index price minus a per-asset haircut, used to compute Total Account Value, margin requirements, and the [[concepts/risk/liquidation|liquidation]] price[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin][^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin][^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-margin]. Collateral simultaneously plays three roles: it is the buffer above maintenance margin (loss-absorbing), the funding source for new initial margin (capacity), and the asset seized by the liquidation engine when that buffer is exhausted (loss-realizing)[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin].

The defining axes that distinguish collateral regimes across venues are: **(a) asset set** (USDC-only vs multi-asset); **(b) haircut function** (none, flat ratio, size-discounted weight, or three-parameter LTV/LT/LF triple); **(c) margin scope** (cross, isolated, strict-isolated, market-flagged isolated); **(d) per-asset capacity caps** (per-user and venue-global); and **(e) rehypothecation** (whether the venue may lend the posted asset). The Variants table below maps the six surveyed venues onto these axes.

Distinguish collateral from two adjacent concepts:

1. **Vault depositor capital** that backstops the venue's solvency on behalf of all traders is treated as `[[concepts/lp/vault]]` deposit, not as user collateral — its loss-socialization, lockup, and operator-share mechanics are filed there. A vault deposit is the depositor's exposure to *other traders'* PnL; collateral is the trader's exposure to *their own* PnL.
2. **Insurance-fund capital** is the residual-loss buffer drawn upon when collateral is fully depleted and book/backstop liquidation cannot absorb the remainder, filed under `[[concepts/risk/insurance-fund-sizing]]`. The IF is a protocol-side resource; collateral is a user-side resource. They meet at the bankruptcy boundary, where the IF takes over what collateral could not cover.

Some venues additionally allow **LP-token-as-collateral** (an LP share or a venue-issued staked token counts toward TAV at a haircut) — a specific case noted below per venue. This is structurally distinct from posting the LP share *as* a vault deposit: when used as collateral, the LP token funds *the holder's own* margin; when used as a vault deposit, it funds the protocol's MM/insurance role.

## Mechanism

### Single-asset (USDC-only) margin
Most venues, in their default configuration, quote, settle, and margin in a single stable unit (USDC or USDT)[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining][^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-margin]. TAV is the USDC quoteBalance plus the marked value of perp positions[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-margin]; no per-asset haircut applies because there is exactly one asset. The single-asset model trades non-USD asset utility for a uniform pricing surface — every margin invariant collapses to a single USDC equation, simplifying both engine implementation and trader mental model.

### Multi-asset margin (MAM)
Non-USDC assets count toward TAV at a per-asset haircut. Lighter formalises three per-asset parameters[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin]:

- **LTV** (Loan-to-Value): fraction counting toward TAV.
- **LT** (Liquidation Threshold): fraction counting toward TALT (Total Account Liquidation Threshold), with the strict ordering `LT ≥ LTV`.
- **LF** (Liquidation Factor): minimum value the user receives per unit of collateral on seizure, with `LF ≥ LT`.

The strict `LF ≥ LT ≥ LTV` ladder is intentional: TAV (the smallest) is conservative for capacity decisions (admit positions); TALT (middle) is the liquidation-trigger health metric; LF (largest) is the user-protection floor on what they recover from a forced collateral sale[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin]. Drift uses an analogous **`assetWeight`** but with a size-discounted form `assetWeight_eff = (1.1 × assetWeight) / (1 + imfFactor × sqrt(size × 10))`[^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin] — coupling the haircut to position-size risk in a single closed-form expression. Aster expresses it as a flat "collateral ratio" — USDF at 99.99% and asBNB at 95%[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn]. All three are the same idea applied at different sophistication levels: a 5% haircut on a 100 USD asset gives 95 USD of margin capacity.

### Isolated vs cross margin
Cross-margin pools all collateral against all positions of the same scope; a winning position subsidises a losing one[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining][^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-margin]. Isolated-margin allocates a slice of collateral to one position, capping the loss at that slice and explicitly disabling cross-subsidy[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining][^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin]. dYdX hardens this further with the `market_type` flag — Isolated markets have segregated collateral pools **and** a per-isolated-market insurance fund, structurally preventing cross-market contagion at both the user-collateral and protocol-IF layers[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-isolated-markets]. Hyperliquid extends the spectrum with **strict-isolated** mode: margin removal is disabled and only releases proportionally on close — the venue can impose this on high-risk markets to prevent traders siphoning margin off an at-risk position[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining]. Lighter scopes isolated margin via a dedicated AllocatedMargin per position, evaluated through the same TAV→MMR→CMR waterfall but on the allocated slice rather than cross collateral[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-liquidations-and-llp-insurance-fund].

### Per-asset position cap
A global cap on how much of asset X can back positions venue-wide. Lighter exposes both a **User Supply Cap** (max one user can hold as margin) and a **Global Supply Cap** (max across all users)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin-collateral-supply-limits]. The two-level cap structure bounds the venue's exposure to a haircut-mispriced asset (global) while preventing single-trader concentration (user) — without throttling per-trader capacity in the common case where individual demand is well below the global ceiling.

### LP-token / staked-token as collateral
Drift's Spot Asset Tier system explicitly classifies tokens into five tiers — Collateral (full privilege) / Protected (collateral, no borrow) / Cross (not collateral, multi-borrow) / Isolated (not collateral, single borrow) / Unlisted (no privilege)[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-market-specs]. Tokens in the Cross/Isolated/Unlisted tiers cannot serve as collateral at all. DRIFT itself acts as collateral only for the Drift Safety Module — DRIFT staked into DSM acts as backstop for bad debt in DRIFT-pair markets only, asset-isolated to DRIFT pairs[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-risk-and-safety-drift-safety-module]. Aster's USDF (yield-bearing stablecoin) and asBNB (BNB liquid staking) are eligible as collateral at venue-set ratios — the explicit design pattern is to let yield-bearing assets earn their native yield while simultaneously serving margin[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn].

### Rehypothecation policy
Whether the venue lends or redeploys posted collateral. Drift's Spot Asset Tiers expose borrow and asset/liability weights — collateral on Drift may be borrowed against the venue's lending book, with strict oracle prices applied (assets use `oracle − confidence`, liabilities use `oracle + confidence`)[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-market-specs][^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin]. Other surveyed venues do not surface explicit rehypothecation policy in primary docs — a material gap for traders evaluating counterparty risk on idle collateral.

### Continuous valuation and oracle dependency
Collateral is not statically valued: every venue marks non-quote collateral on every block / fill / oracle update. Lighter formalises the dependency in `TAV(USDC) = PortfolioBalance(USDC) + Σ_j LTV_j · MarginBalance_j · IndexPrice_j` and the parallel `TALT(USDC) = PortfolioBalance(USDC) + Σ_j LT_j · MarginBalance_j · IndexPrice_j`[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin]. Both quantities are functions of `IndexPrice_j` — an oracle output — so collateral health is continuously coupled to oracle integrity. Drift hardens this coupling with strict oracle prices on the spot-margin path (assets use `oracle − confidence`, liabilities use `oracle + confidence`)[^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin] and a 10% oracle-mark divergence band that pauses risk-increasing actions when the band is breached[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-risk-and-safety-risk-parameters]. The collateral concept therefore inherits oracle-attack risk: a manipulated oracle does not just mis-price a position — it mis-prices the buffer that would otherwise absorb the manipulation's impact.

## Edge cases

- **Aster collateral-yield stacking.** Aster's Trade & Earn uses Multi-Asset Mode specifically to let yield-bearing assets (USDF, asBNB) earn their native yield while simultaneously acting as collateral — collateral ratios 99.99% / 95% reflect a near-zero haircut on USDF and a ~5% haircut on asBNB[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn]. The mode is also the structural reason a 100,000 USDF holdings cap exists: it bounds the venue's USDF mark-to-stable risk[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn].
- **Drift multi-asset Insurance Fund vs multi-asset collateral.** Drift's IF is multi-asset (separate USDC / BTC / ETH / SOL pools) but each pool only covers same-denomination liabilities[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-insurance-fund]. This is structurally distinct from multi-asset collateral on the user side — a user can post BTC as collateral against a perp position, but if that position generates USDC bad debt, it draws from the USDC IF, not from the BTC IF. Asset-isolation is asymmetric across the user/protocol boundary.
- **Lighter ETH-as-collateral live-status dispute.** The `trading-multi-asset-margin` page describes ETH as "the first non-USDC asset added"[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin] (implying live), while the `collateral-supply-limits` page renders the ETH global cap as "ETH as collateral is coming soon" (implying not live)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-multi-asset-margin-collateral-supply-limits]. The two pages are simultaneously published and mutually inconsistent at the 2026-04-28 mirror; resolution pending — see [[entities/perpdex/lighter]]#disputed-claims and the existing entry in `open-questions.md`.
- **Orderly per-chain divergence.** Orderly's multi-collateral surface exists only on Ethereum mainnet (USDC, USDT, YUSD, WBTC, USD1)[^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-addresses]; other chains (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Sei) accept USDC only[^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-trading-fees]. A user's effective collateral set is therefore a function of deposit chain — a constraint not captured in single-account abstractions.
- **dYdX USDC-only with structural isolation.** dYdX v4 quotes, settles, and margins exclusively in USDC[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-margin]; multi-asset diversity is achieved at the **account topology** layer (cross subaccount + up to 128,000 isolated subaccounts per address[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-isolated-markets]) rather than at the asset layer. This trades non-USD asset utility for a clean per-market loss-containment model: an isolated market's collateral never subsidises another market.
- **Drift size-discounted asset weight.** Unlike fixed haircuts on Aster/Lighter, Drift continuously degrades collateral capacity as position size grows: a $10 of collateral marked at `assetWeight=0.9` and `imfFactor=0.001` falls toward zero margin capacity as size scales — coupling collateral haircut to position-size risk in one formula[^drift-sdk-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-margin][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-risk-and-safety-risk-parameters]. The same `imfFactor` also enters the liability-side weighting via `max(0.8 × wgt + sqrt(size) × imf_factor, wgt)`, so a single risk parameter controls both the asset-side capacity and the borrow-side cost as size scales[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-risk-and-safety-risk-parameters].
- **Hyperliquid transfer-margin floor.** When transferring margin out of a cross account (withdrawal, transfer to spot wallet, isolated-position margin reduction), the engine enforces `transfer_margin_required = max(initial_margin_required, 0.1 × total_position_value)`[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-margining]. The 10% floor is independent of user-set leverage — it prevents a trader from bringing their account to the bare minimum-margin state shortly before a margin run, by forcing a buffer above naive `initial_margin_required` at withdrawal time. This is a collateral-egress invariant distinct from the entry-side initial-margin check.
- **Rehypothecation transparency gap.** Of the six venues surveyed, only Drift exposes a documented borrow/lend layer that operates on user-posted collateral via the Spot Asset Tier system[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-market-specs]. Hyperliquid, Aster, Lighter, Orderly, and dYdX do not surface a rehypothecation policy in primary docs — the absence is itself the data point. For traders, this means idle collateral on five of six venues has an unspecified counterparty risk profile; for the venues, it is an opportunity for explicit policy disclosure on the next docs refresh.
