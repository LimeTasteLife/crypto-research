# Maker rebate

## Definition

A **maker rebate** is a *negative* maker fee — the venue pays the passive (resting) side of a trade for adding liquidity, instead of charging it[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate][^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards]. A maker order is one that rests on the book before being filled (typically gated by a `post-only` flag); a taker order matches immediately and pays the taker fee[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate]. The rebate is the venue's standalone, on-book economic mechanism for compensating liquidity provision.

The rebate is **not** the same as adjacent mechanisms it is often confused with:

- **Maker fee.** A non-negative charge to the maker side; documented elsewhere as the zero-or-positive complement of the rebate (e.g. Lighter Premium 0.0040% maker[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-trading-fees]; Aster 0% maker on USDT-perps[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees]).
- **MM agreement rebate.** Off-chain, bilaterally-negotiated payments to a named market-making firm under an explicit contract — see [[concepts/mm-agreement/quote-obligation]]. The on-book maker rebate covered here is permissionless and applies to anyone who hits the tier thresholds.
- **Volume-tier discount.** A non-negative reduction in maker (or taker) fee as volume grows; the rebate is the special case where the post-discount maker rate crosses zero into negative territory[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards][^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees].
- **Funding-rate rebate.** A different cash-flow channel that returns a fraction of *funding paid* (not trading fee) to the funding-paying side — see [[concepts/fee-model/funding-rate]] and the Lighter funding-rate-rebates parameter[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-funding-funding-rate-rebates] for the canonical example.

## Mechanism

**Funding source.** The rebate has to be paid from somewhere. Three documented sources across the surveyed venues:

- **Taker-fee subsidy** (cross-side). The taker-side fee is set high enough that a fraction can be redirected to the maker. Hyperliquid pays tier-1+ rebates of −0.001%/−0.002%/−0.003% against a tier-0 perps taker of 0.045%, with the rebate paid continuously per trade directly to the trading wallet[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees]. dYdX v4's tier-7 −1.1 bps maker rebate is funded by the 2.5 bps taker fee charged at the same tier; the staking discount overlay applies only to net-positive trading fees, so a maker who is already in rebate territory gets no further multiplicative discount on the rebate itself[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards].
- **Pool budget.** Drift pays a flat −0.25 bps base rebate from a documented "Rebate Pool" capped at 2/3 of pool balance, with a stated sunset clause (the program is explicitly subject to change)[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-trading-fees]. The rebate scales as `base × (1 + staking_boost)` rather than as a separate ladder[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-trading-fees].
- **MM-program budget** (separate from base fee schedule). Aster's MM1/MM2/MM3 ladder pays −0.25 / −0.35 / −0.5 bps rebate to whitelisted addresses; the rebate is computed and disbursed hourly, displayed as 0 in the trading UI but settled separately and visible in transaction history under Aster Perpetual[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program]. Aster reserves discretion to modify, suspend, or terminate the program at any time, and onboarding requires ≥$100M USD monthly futures volume on any platform plus IP-whitelisting[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program].

A fourth pattern — *no on-book maker rebate at all* — appears at Orderly, where the base maker fee is 0 bps across all Builder Staking tiers and the on-chain rebate decision is delegated upward to the Builder layer; permissionless-listing Builders earn 50% of trading-fee + 100% of liquidation-fee revenue from their own markets and can rebate the user from that margin if they choose[^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-trading-fees][^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-custom-fees]. Lighter sits in a similar space: Premium-track maker fees stay non-negative even at the top staking tier (0.0028% maker)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-trading-fees], and the documented liquidity-side payout is the LP Program — a weekly reward pool distributed against randomized order-book snapshots, not a per-trade negative fee[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-liquidity-partner-program].

**Tier mechanics.** Three tiering schemes appear in the wild:

- **Volume-share** (relative). Hyperliquid keys tier on share of platform-wide 14d weighted maker volume (>0.5% / >1.5% / >3.0%) — as platform volume grows, absolute volume needed to keep a tier scales with it, so the rebate budget is implicitly capped at three slots in each share band[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees]. The 14d weight is `14d_perps + 2 × 14d_spot` and sub-account volume aggregates to the master, but vault volume is treated separately[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees].
- **Volume-absolute** (rolling lookback). dYdX v4 uses 30-day trailing volume across sub-accounts and all markets, with seven tiers from <$1M to ≥$200M; the maker rate crosses zero at tier 5 (≥$50M) and reaches its top rebate at tier 7 (≥$200M, −1.1 bps)[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards]. The look-back window is governance-adjustable in seconds via the Stats Module[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-modules-governance-fee-tiers]. Aster MM tiers gate on rolling-14d volume **or** maker-volume-share (whichever qualifies first)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program].
- **Stake-scaled boost** (token-gated). Drift overlays a +5% to +40% multiplicative boost on the −0.25 bps base, scaled to DRIFT staked (1k / 10k / 50k / 100k / 250k); the boost stacks on the volume-tier base rather than replacing it[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-trading-trading-fees]. Lighter's Premium-track maker fee shrinks (rather than crossing into negative) as LIT staked grows along an 8-tier ladder capped at 30% discount, and the discount applies at the L1-address level — the main account and all sub-accounts share a single tier determined by aggregated LIT stake[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-trading-fees].

**Markout-adjusted rebate (PIII pattern).** None of the surveyed venues publishes a primary-doc rebate that explicitly segregates by markout window or toxic-flow class[^hl-docs-2026-04-27-trading-fees][^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate][^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program][^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-trading-fees][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-trading-fees]. The closest documented proxies for adverse-selection screening are quoting-quality rules (Aster's ≥70% UTC-day quoting standard with ≥0.5s order-live requirement and pair-specific spread caps)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program] and AMM-fill carve-outs (Drift excludes maker-vs-AMM fills from rebate eligibility, redirecting rebates only toward user-counterparty flow)[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-about-v3-decentralized-orderbook]. Cross-venue, this is a documented gap (frame A2/PIII / A4/PIII — see entity pages for [[entities/perpdex/hyperliquid]], [[entities/perpdex/orderly]], [[entities/perpdex/dydx]], [[entities/perpdex/lighter]]).

**Anti-abuse.** Two documented countermeasures recur:

- **Self-match prevention.** Lighter cancels the resting same-account maker order when a same-account taker would self-cross — a "cancel-resting" STP variant that prevents zero-position-change rebate harvesting[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-trading-self-trade-prevention]. Self-trade volume is also categorically excluded from referral attribution on Aster (and, by construction, from Drift's referrer rewards because referral cannot be cross-account)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn].
- **Quoting-quality gate.** Aster disqualifies a pair from preferential MM fees the next day if the qualifying-percentage drops below 70%, treating it as a wash-quote screen[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program].

## Edge cases

- **Eligibility gate via gated track (Lighter).** A Lighter user cannot earn even a positive maker rebate-adjacent payout (LP Program weekly reward pool) on a Standard account — Premium account opt-in is a hard precondition[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-liquidity-partner-program][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Premium itself is gated by paying the regular fee schedule, so the rebate channel costs maker fee plus LIT exposure to access.
- **Protocol-vault MM exclusion (Lighter LLP).** When the protocol's own LLP qualifies for MM points under the Points Program, its share is rescaled out and redistributed to other participants so the weekly MM pool stays at 50,000 — preventing a venue-owned MM from cannibalizing third-party MM rewards while keeping the scoring rule symmetric[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].
- **AMM-fill carve-out (Drift).** A `post-only` maker order that fills against Drift's AMM is *not* eligible for the −0.25 bps rebate; only maker-vs-user fills earn it[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-about-v3-decentralized-orderbook]. This redirects rebate dollars away from the protocol-as-counterparty leg and toward the user-counterparty leg, which is the closest the surveyed set comes to a documented adverse-selection-aware rebate adjustment.
- **No on-book rebate at the venue layer (Orderly).** Orderly's base maker fee is 0 bps across all Builder Staking tiers; the venue does not pay a negative on-book maker rate at all[^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-trading-fees]. Builders capture 50% of trading-fee + 100% of liquidation-fee revenue from their own permissionless markets and may set the user-facing fee schedule themselves[^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-trading-fees][^orderly-docs-2026-04-29-custom-fees] — the rebate decision is delegated up to the Builder rather than centralized.
- **Discretion / sunset clause.** Drift explicitly disclaims permanence of the maker-rebate program[^drift-docs-2026-04-28-protocol-market-makers-maker-fee-rebate]; Aster reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the MM program at any time[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program]. The rebate is therefore a discretionary venue commitment, not a binding agreement, on these venues.
