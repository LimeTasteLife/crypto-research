# Protocol Revenue Sharing

Source: https://docs.dydx.community/dydx/modules/distribution/protocol-revenue-sharing

On August 12, 2024, dYdX Trading Inc. announced dYdX Unlimited, software featuring significant improvements to the dYdX Chain open-source software, including potential revenue sharing, among other things.

Protocol Revenue Sharing enables the dYdX community to use `MsgUpdateUnconditionalRevShareConfig` to adjust the allocation of net revenue received by the protocol with addresses other than the Distribution module account (which distributes to validators, stakers, and community pool).

On November 13, 2025, the dYdX community approved the following distribution:

- 75% revenue sharing to the Buyback Program,
- 5% to Treasury SubDAO,
- 5% to MegaVault, and
- 15% to the Distribution module account.
