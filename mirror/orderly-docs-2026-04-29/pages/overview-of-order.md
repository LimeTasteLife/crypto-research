# Overview of $ORDER

$ORDER is the native token of Orderly Network. It aligns incentives across the ecosystem — rewarding participants who provide liquidity, trade actively, build products, and govern the protocol.

## Ecosystem participants

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Market makers | Provide liquidity by placing buy and sell orders. Earn trading profits and liquidity provision rewards in $ORDER. |
| Traders | Buy and sell assets on Orderly, contributing to volume and price discovery. |
| $ORDER holders | Stake tokens to earn a share of protocol revenue and participate in governance. |
| Builders | Develop front-end DEXs and apps on Orderly's infrastructure. May receive builder grants. |

## Token utility

1. Governance — Staked $ORDER grants voting rights over protocol decisions as governance is formalized.
2. Earn VALOR — VALOR measures staking position (amount and duration) and entitles to a share of the protocol treasury.
3. Trading reward boost — Staking increases share of trading rewards.
4. Market making reward boost — Staking increases share of MM rewards.
5. Future utilities — Additional uses with upcoming products.
