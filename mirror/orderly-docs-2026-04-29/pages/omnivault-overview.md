# OmniVault Overview

Orderly OmniVault is Orderly's flagship strategy vault. It is an omnichain and permissionless strategy vault designed to leverage protocol stakeholder participation to amplify liquidity and enhance on-chain trading experience across tradable markets.

Orderly collaborates with industry-leading market makers as Strategy Providers (SP), starting with Kronos Research. SPs execute efficient market-making strategies through utilization and management of OmniVault assets.

Users deposit USDC into the OmniVault from any supported blockchain network and become protocol liquidity providers, earning a proportional share of OmniVault's PnL from professional market-making strategies.

## Security

Orderly has completed two independent audits on strategy vault smart contracts. Audit reports publicly available on GitHub.
