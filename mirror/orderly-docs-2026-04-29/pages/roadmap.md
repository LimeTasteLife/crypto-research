# Product Roadmap (2026 H1)

## Growth

- Point Module for Orderly One: unified points system across campaigns.
- Multi-Level Referral: extends referral to multiple tiers.
- Orderly Monthly Competition: recurring monthly trading competition.
- Expanded RWA Market: broadens tradable asset universe with RWA-linked perps.

## Trading capabilities

- Isolated Margin: per-position margin for independent leverage.
- Permissionless Listing: self-service market listing for qualified builders.

## Platform & developer

- System Performance Upgrade: lower latency, higher throughput.
- Core SDK Enhancements: cleaner APIs, richer primitives.
- Mobile SDK: iOS and Android.

## Vault upgrades

- Permissionless Vault: open vault creation to any strategist.
- Non-USDC Vault: ETH, BTC, LST deposits.
