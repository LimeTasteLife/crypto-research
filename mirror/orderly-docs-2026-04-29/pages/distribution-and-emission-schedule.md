## Emission schedule

### Community (55%)

Epochs: 1 epoch = 14 days. Rewards unlock over 200 epochs (~7 years).

- Retroactive Airdrop (13.3% / 133M $ORDER): Unlocked at TGE.
- Ecosystem Incentives (25% / 250M $ORDER): Distributed based on trading volume, user engagement, ecosystem growth.
- Builder Rewards (8.35% / 83.5M $ORDER): Grants for builders based on volume, DAU, new users.
- Future Product Launches (8.35% / 83.5M $ORDER): Locked until usage formally established.

### Strategic Investors (15% / 150M $ORDER)

Locked 6 months after TGE, followed by 3.5-year linear vesting.

### Team & Advisors (20% / 200M $ORDER)

1-year cliff (25% unlocks), followed by 3-year linear vesting.

### Foundation (10% / 100M $ORDER)

- 38.5M $ORDER available post-TGE for liquidity.
- 12.575M $ORDER for marketing partnerships over 2 years.
- Remaining in reserve. Foundation goal: transfer full control to DAO.
