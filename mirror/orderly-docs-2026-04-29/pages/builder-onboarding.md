# Builder Onboarding

Use this page when your team is planning to launch its own trading frontend on Orderly and onboard its own users.

## Launch paths

### Option A: Orderly One
Faster path. Lower-code route with branded trading experience.

### Option B: Custom SDK/API integration
Full control over product design, frontend behavior, and integration logic.

## Builder setup

1. Contact Orderly through official channels.
2. Provide: Builder ID, Builder name, Initial fee configuration, Launch path.
3. Orderly provisions builder profile and permissions.
4. Review fee model — Builders define user-facing fee, Orderly charges base fee underneath, gap is builder's margin.
5. Register admin account.

## Estimated timeline

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Choose path + info | Immediate |
| Builder profile creation | 1-2 business days |
| Admin registration + linkage | 2-3 business days |

Total: ~3-5 business days.
