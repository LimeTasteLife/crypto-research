# Security

## Audits

Orderly has been audited by multiple security firms, including Halborn, Zellic, Guardian Audits, and several independent consultants. All audit reports can be found on GitHub.

Trusted and incubated by WOO Network.

## Governance

Updates to the protocol are governed and approved by a multi-sig approver mechanism with signers who have the deepest interest in the success of Orderly.
