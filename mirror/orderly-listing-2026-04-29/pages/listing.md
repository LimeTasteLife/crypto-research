1. **Price Oracle Availability**: 3+ price oracles available. Compatible sources: Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, BingX, Gate.io, Kucoin, HTX, MEXC, WOO X.

2. **Market Capitalization**: $20M or above Market Cap.

3. **Liquidity Support**: Must provide own liquidity provider with specific performance metrics.
