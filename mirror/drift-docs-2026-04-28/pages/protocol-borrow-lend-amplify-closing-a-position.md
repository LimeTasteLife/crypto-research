# Closing a Position

## Reduce position size

You can reduce position size with clicking [Withdraw] and reduce the deposited amount to your intended amount. Drift Amplify will automatically adjust your borrowing position to meet your intended position size.

## Unwind a position

Close a position by withdrawing 100% of the deposited amount.
