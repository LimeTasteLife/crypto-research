# Protocol Revenue Sharing

## TL;DR
Critical tokenomics page. dYdX Unlimited (Aug 12, 2024 announcement) introduced Protocol Revenue Sharing via `MsgUpdateUnconditionalRevShareConfig`. Current allocation (approved by community Nov 13, 2025): 75% to Buyback Program, 5% to Treasury SubDAO, 5% to MegaVault, 15% to the Distribution module account (which then splits to validators/stakers/community pool).
