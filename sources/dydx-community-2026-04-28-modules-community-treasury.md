# Community Treasury

## TL;DR
The Community Treasury (`dydx15ztc7xy42tn2ukkc0qjthkucw9ac63pgp70urn`) is a community-controlled module account; DYDX vests to it continuously until August 3, 2026. Currently 26.1% (261,133,225 DYDX) is allocated to it, vesting at ~2.04 DYDX/second from the Community Vester (`dydx1wxje320an3karyc6mjw4zghs300dmrjkwn7xtk`). Initially 5% (50M DYDX) at genesis, with 766,703 DYDX/epoch original vesting; today 3,787,251 DYDX/epoch (3,020,548 DYDX increase due to 6 DIPs cutting other reward streams). DIP 29 also migrated 80,492,270 DYDX from v3 Rewards Treasury + Community Treasury to v4 Community Treasury. Spending requires a Community Spending governance proposal.
