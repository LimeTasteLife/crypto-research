# Perpetuals

## TL;DR
Aster Perpetuals ("Perps") is the platform's decentralized perpetual contracts product and the core of Aster, available on all supported chains. The product is positioned with two headline features: a multi-asset margin model (trade using a variety of cryptocurrencies as collateral) and "lower-than-CEX" advertised fees of 0.01% maker / 0.035% taker. The page also references "Aster Pro mode," indicating Pro is the advanced trading interface for Perps. The page acts as a hub for downstream documentation on trading mechanics, margin, and risk management.
