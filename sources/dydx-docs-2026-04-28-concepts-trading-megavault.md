# MegaVault

## TL;DR
MegaVault is dYdX's protocol-as-MM vault: users deposit USDC, the vault runs automated AMM strategies across multiple markets via per-market sub-vaults. Yield comes from PnL on market-making positions, a share of trading fees, and funding payments. An operator (governance-elected) tunes parameters and reallocates between sub-vaults; future versions may automate this. Withdrawals are on-demand but slippage applies (especially with leverage or large withdrawals). Yield is not guaranteed; depositors bear market risk including negative trading PnL.
