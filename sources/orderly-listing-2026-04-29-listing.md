# Orderly Network Listing

## TL;DR
Standard listing criteria: (1) 3+ price oracles from supported CEX sources (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, BingX, Gate.io, Kucoin, HTX, MEXC, WOO X), (2) $20M+ market cap, (3) own liquidity provider with performance metrics. Liquidity requirements: ±2% depth ≥20K, ≥15 levels, spread ≤50 bps, uptime ≥90%. Listing referral bonus: $2,000 fixed + 30% of project marketing budget (capped $10,000). Listed projects get access to 30+ omnichain DEXs and 300K+ social media users.
