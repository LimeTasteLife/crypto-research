# DYDX Token Allocation

## TL;DR
1B DYDX minted on Aug 3, 2021, vesting over 5 years. Current allocation: 50% community (26.1% Treasury, 14.5% Trading Rewards, 5% retroactive mining, 3.3% LP Rewards, 0.6% USDC LP staking, 0.5% safety pool), 27.7% past investors, 15.3% founders/employees, 7.0% future employees. The page also lists initial vs current splits — the Trading Rewards allocation was cut from 25% via DIPs 16/20/29; LP Rewards cut via DIP 24/29; USDC and Safety pool rewards set to 0 via DIPs 14/17. Investor lockup amended Jan 2023: 30% release Dec 2023, then equal monthly schedule through June 2026.
