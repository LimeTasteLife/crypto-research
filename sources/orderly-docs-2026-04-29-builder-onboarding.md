# Builder Onboarding

## TL;DR
Describes the two launch paths for builders integrating with Orderly: Option A (Orderly One, lower-code branded trading experience) and Option B (custom SDK/API integration with full control). Setup involves contacting Orderly, providing builder profile details (ID, name, fee config, launch path), and Orderly provisioning the profile. Total setup time is ~3-5 business days. Builders define user-facing fees; Orderly charges a base fee underneath; the gap is the builder's margin.
