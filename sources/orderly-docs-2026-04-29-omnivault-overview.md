# OmniVault Overview

## TL;DR
OmniVault is Orderly's flagship omnichain strategy vault. Industry-leading market makers act as Strategy Providers (SPs), starting with Kronos Research. Users deposit USDC from any supported chain (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Sei) and earn proportional share of PnL from professional market-making strategies. Two independent audits completed on vault smart contracts. Smart contract address: 0x70fe7d65ac7c1a1732f64d2e6fc0e33622d0c991.
