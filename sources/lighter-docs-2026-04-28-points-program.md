# Points Program

## TL;DR
Top-level page for Lighter's Points Program. Season 1 ended on 2025-09-30 with the final Private Beta distribution; Season 2 distributes points every Friday and may be adjusted at the team's discretion. Points reward organic UI/API trading; Sybil, self-trading, and similar behavior do not earn. Page is the entry point that links to Market Makers and Retail subpages.
