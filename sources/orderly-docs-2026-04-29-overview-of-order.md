# Overview of $ORDER

## TL;DR
$ORDER is Orderly Network's native token aligning incentives across market makers, traders, holders, and builders. Five utilities: (1) governance voting rights as governance is formalized, (2) VALOR earning for staking (share of protocol treasury), (3) trading reward boost, (4) market making reward boost, (5) future utilities. Ecosystem participants include market makers (liquidity + $ORDER rewards), traders (volume + price discovery), $ORDER holders (stake for revenue share + governance), and builders (front-end DEXs, may receive grants).
