# Security

## TL;DR
Orderly has been audited by Halborn, Zellic, Guardian Audits, and independent consultants; reports available on GitHub. The protocol is trusted and incubated by WOO Network. Governance updates are approved by a multi-sig mechanism with signers who have the "deepest interest in the success of Orderly." Page is sparse — no audit dates, scope detail, bug bounty, or incident response policy.
