# What is Aster?

## TL;DR
Aster positions itself as a privacy-focused decentralized perp DEX offering perpetual markets on crypto, stocks, and commodities. Its key differentiation is encrypted order flow: every order is encrypted before reaching the chain and only decrypted at execution, hiding position size, entry, and liquidation level from on-chain observers — a direct response to position-hunting on transparent perpdexes. Aster runs on its own L1 (Aster Chain) targeting 100,000+ TPS and 50ms block latency. The product surface spans four trading types — order-book Perpetuals, AMM-style Shield Mode (with built-in privacy and higher leverage), 1001x (one-click MEV-resistant on-chain perps), and Spot — plus Aster Earn yield products including the BNB liquid staking derivative asBNB and the yield-bearing stablecoin USDF.
