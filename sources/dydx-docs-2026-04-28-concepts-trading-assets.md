# Perpetuals and Assets

## TL;DR
Definition page for perpetuals (no expiry, funding-rate-anchored to spot) and dYdX's collateral model. Principal assets on dYdX are USDC and the dYdX token. Collateral is used for opening/maintaining positions, satisfying margin requirements, fee payments, and rewards. Definition-heavy page with no parameter values.
