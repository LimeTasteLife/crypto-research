# Distribution and Emission Schedule

## TL;DR
$ORDER max supply is 1B, ERC-20 on Ethereum and OFT on other chains via LayerZero. Allocation: Community 55% (Retroactive Airdrop 13.3%, Ecosystem Incentives 25%, Builder Rewards 8.35%, Future Product Launches 8.35%), Strategic Investors 15%, Team & Advisors 20%, Foundation 10%. Emission over 200 epochs (~7 years, 1 epoch = 14 days). Investor tokens: 6-month lock + 3.5-year linear vesting. Team: 1-year cliff (25% unlock) + 3-year linear. Foundation: 38.5M post-TGE for liquidity. Goal: transfer full control to DAO.
