# Supported Chains

## TL;DR
Orderly supports 16 EVM chains (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Mantle, Ethereum, SEI, Avalanche, Morph, Sonic, Story, Mode, Plume, Abstract, BNB Smart Chain, Monad) and 1 non-EVM chain (Solana). Users can deposit/withdraw from any supported chain to trade on Orderly's unified orderbook.
