# Retail (Points Program)

## TL;DR
For Season 2, 200,000 retail points per week distribute every Friday, covering Wed–Tue activity. Points reward Volume, Open Interest, Fundings, Liquidations/Deleverages, and PnL with non-linear scaling and quality-favoring multipliers. Premium accounts may receive heavier weighting (especially on PnL). Markets are not equally weighted. Sybil detection is mixed automated/semi-automated; up to 10 accounts per user are tolerated without penalty. Specific anti-farming metrics are intentionally undisclosed.
