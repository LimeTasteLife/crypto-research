# Referral Program

## TL;DR
Aster's referral programme pays a default 10% commission of referred-user trading fees, configurably split between the referrer and the referee (e.g., 5%/5% as a fee discount). High-volume affiliates can apply for upgraded rates: 20% commission from VIP 1 referees and 10% from VIP 2+. Rebates settle in real time and credit to the referrer's Aster account by the next day; rebate rates are calculated daily at 00:00 UTC and updated at 09:00 UTC. Rewards are paid in the same asset the referee used to pay fees. Referral relationships are valid for 365 days by default (Aster may extend at its discretion). Liquidation fees, self-trade volume, and zero-fee trade volume are excluded from commission calculations. Self-referrals trigger full reward revocation. Aster reserves unilateral right to modify rates, rules, or cancel the programme.
