# Trading Rewards

## TL;DR
Trading rewards target traders specifically (vs. staking rewards for validators/stakers). Per-block, per-trade reward distributed in NATIVE_TOKEN dYdX. Source: configured Rewards Treasury (T). Goals: incentivize trading, limit overspending, deterministic/per-block, capped by paid fees, prevent self-trading profit. Two formulas: `taker_reward = taker_volume × (taker_fee_rate − max_maker_rebate − max_affiliate_taker_fee_share) × C × (1 − protocol_revenue_share_rate)` and `maker_reward = maker_volume × (positive_maker_fees) × C × (1 − protocol_revenue_share_rate)`. Affiliate share is 0 if 30d volume > $50M, else 50% × taker_fee_rate. Total trading rewards capped at 90% of fill's net trading fees. C currently set to 0 by governance — traders get rebates directly. Note conflict with community-docs which says C-based trading rewards have been entirely removed and consolidated under Surge Program.

## Conflicts raised
- This page describes Trading Rewards as a live mechanism with C-based formula, while `dydx-community-2026-04-28-modules-trading-rewards`#c states protocol-level trading rewards have been removed and consolidated under the Surge Program. The two docs disagree on whether trading rewards still exist at the protocol level; this docs.dydx.xyz page also notes "C factor has been set to 0" inline (claim #c11), suggesting effective deprecation while the formula remains documented. Phase 2 should reconcile.
