# Unified Trading Accounts

## TL;DR
Lighter offers two account models. The Unified Trading Account (UTA) provides unified margin across spot and perpetual USDC balances and is described as the first step toward supporting additional spot assets as collateral for perpetual markets — i.e. the precursor to multi-asset margin. The Simple Trading Account keeps perpetual and spot balances strictly separated; perp positions can only use settlement assets (USDC) as collateral, and cross-margining is restricted to perpetuals only and does not extend to spot. The page is short but architecturally important: it defines the account-level boundary between Lighter's two collateral worlds.
