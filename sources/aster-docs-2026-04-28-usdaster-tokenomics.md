# Tokenomics

## TL;DR
$ASTER total supply is 8,000,000,000 (implied: 53.5% = 4.28B). Distribution: Airdrop 53.5%, Ecosystem & Community 30%, Treasury 7%, Team 5%, Liquidity & Listings 4.5%. 8.8% (704M) unlocks immediately at TGE for Aster Spectra and Aster Gems participants. The Airdrop allocation releases gradually over ~80 months (~7 years). The Team allocation has a 12-month cliff then 40 months of linear vesting at 10M $ASTER per month. The Ecosystem & Community linear vesting was active for only 4 months (Oct 2025 – Jan 2026) before being replaced by a staking emission model — staking rewards are now the sole active use of that bucket. Treasury (7%) is fully locked at TGE and only unlocks via governance approval. Protocol revenue is partially redirected to $ASTER buybacks via the Aster Foundation and to governance rewards. APX holders can swap to $ASTER at a decreasing rate; unclaimed tokens redirect back to the Airdrop pool.
