# Governance Adjustable Parameters

## TL;DR
Index page enumerating the categories of dYdX governance-adjustable parameters: Trading Stats (look-back window), Fee Tiers, Trading Core (insurance fund + liquidations config), Markets (Prices module + liquidity tier), Perpetual (funding rate + epoch info), CLOB (order creation/placement/cancellations), Updating Live Markets (Msg* methods), Safety (spam mitigation).
