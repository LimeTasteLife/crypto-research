# Audit Reports

## TL;DR
Aster publishes seven audit reports covering distinct product surfaces: AsterVault (Sept 2024), AsterEarn (Sept 2024), asBNB (twin audits — Salus Security and PeckShield, both Dec 2024), USDF (PeckShield), USDF + asUSDF (HALBORN, listed as "Astherus / SSC"), and asCAKE (Salus Security, Dec 2024). Audit firms used are Salus Security, PeckShield, and HALBORN. Notably the "what is Aster?" page emphasises the encrypted-order privacy claim and Aster Chain L1 design — but no audit report on this page covers the Aster Chain L1 itself or the perp matching engine, only the Vault, Earn, and stablecoin contracts. All listed reports are dated 2024, which precedes the 2025 product timeline implied by other docs (TGE, asUSDF emission changes).
