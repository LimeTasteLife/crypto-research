# Affiliate Program

## TL;DR
A3 mechanism #2 — Affiliate Program. Lifetime commission model paid in USDC on-chain. Two tiers: VIP (≤50% of referred user taker fees, $10,000/referral/30d cap) and Standard (≤15% taker fees, $3,000/referral/30d cap). Volume gate: $10,000 in trading volume required before the affiliate link unlocks. CryptoLearningClub onboarded by dYdX Grants to manage VIP — they submit on-chain proposals to whitelist new VIPs / remove underperformers. VIP eligibility excludes professional MMs and trading firms; requires track record + 30K+ social-media followers + notable social influence.
