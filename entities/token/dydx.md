# DYDX

> dYdX Chain's L1 native token for governance, staking, and fee discounts.

## Asset profile
- DYDX is dYdX Chain's L1 native token, adopted by the dYdX community via Snapshot + on-chain governance[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-start-here-dydx-token]
- Three documented purposes: governance (proposing/voting on changes), security (staking weight raises attack cost), staking (delegators run or delegate to validators)[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-start-here-dydx-token]
- 1B supply minted Aug 3, 2021; 5y vesting; 50% community / 27.7% past investors / 15.3% founders+employees / 7.0% future employees[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-start-here-dydx-token-allocation]
- Initial transfer restrictions lifted Sep 8, 2021; investor lockup amended Jan 25, 2023 with final 10% releasing Jul 2025-Jun 2026[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-start-here-dydx-token-allocation]
- Drives 5-tier-group fee discount on dYdX v4 (25-50% off net positive trading fees; only bonded DYDX counts)[^dydx-docs-2026-04-28-concepts-trading-rewards]
- Per Nov 13, 2025 community vote, 75% of net protocol revenue allocated to Buyback Program (acquires DYDX from market)[^dydx-community-2026-04-28-modules-distribution-protocol-revenue-sharing]

## Snapshot
