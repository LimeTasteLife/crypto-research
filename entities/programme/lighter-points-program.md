# Lighter Points Program

> Lighter's two-track (retail + market-maker) discretionary weekly points distribution. Season 2 is currently live with an implied 250k weekly pool (200k retail + 50k MM); the 250k figure is implicit and Disputed.

## Snapshot
- Operator: Lighter team / Lighter protocol[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]
- Documented seasons: Season 1 ended 2025-09-30 with the final Private Beta distribution; Season 2 is currently active[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program]
- Cadence: weekly (every Friday) for retail; MM cadence implicit but consistent with weekly[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]
- Tracks: Retail (organic UI/API trading) + Market Makers (Premium-only liquidity provision)
- Hosted exchange: [[entities/perpdex/lighter]]

## Mechanism

**Two-track design.** Lighter splits points across a Retail track and a Market Maker track. Retail receives 200,000 points per week distributed every Friday[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Market-making activity receives 20% of total allocated points, equal to 50,000 points; the implied total pool is 250,000 (20% = 50,000)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. The 250k weekly arithmetic is implicit — see Disputed claims.

**Season cadence.** Lighter operates the program in discrete seasons (Season 1, Season 2)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program]. Season 1 ended with the final Private Beta distribution on 2025-09-30[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program]. Season 2 distributes points every Friday and may be adjusted at the team's discretion[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program]. Each weekly retail distribution covers activity from Wednesday (inclusive) through Tuesday (inclusive)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].

**Retail formula.** Retail-track points are computed across multiple categories: Volume, Open Interest, Fundings, Liquidations & Deleverages, and PnL[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Scaling factors favor traders with higher trading quality, depending on the category[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Premium-account status impacts the weight of metrics; PnL generated by Premium accounts may receive more points[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Some categories are evaluated per-market and others globally; some daily and others weekly[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Points are not necessarily linear in the underlying metric — 2× the volume may yield ~3× or ~1.5× the points (extends beyond volume)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Markets are not weighted equally for retail points; e.g. $100 of BTC volume does not yield the same points as $100 of HYPE volume[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail]. Intentionally losing money or being liquidated is not beneficial for points accrual[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].

**Market-Maker formula.** Only Premium accounts are eligible to earn MM points[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Volume score formula: `score = volume + max(0, (volume − 2.5B) × 0.25)` — i.e. a 25% bonus to weekly maker volume above $2.5B[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Volume points are computed once for the entire week, not daily; $1 of volume on the first day has the same weight as $1 on the last day; all markets are weighted equally for the volume-based score[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].

Liquidity-provision points use random order-book snapshots, with users scored proportionally to their share of liquidity[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. At each snapshot, several metrics evaluate the top $10k / $30k / $100k / $300k / … of liquidity and the top 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 bps from mid-price[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Roughly 6–10 metrics per market are used, each carrying the same weight, computed separately for bid and ask sides[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Market weight for liquidity points blends Lighter's own data with adjusted external-exchange volume and open-interest multipliers, recomputed hourly; the weighting shifts toward heavily-traded tokens (BTC, ETH, SOL) and toward tokens popular elsewhere but not yet active on Lighter[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. A per-snapshot volatility multiplier increases the weight of high-volatility snapshots to incentivise quoting through volatility[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. Liquidity-provision total scores are calculated daily because the volatility multiplier varies snapshot by snapshot[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].

**Daily floor and cap (weekly-distribution sanity).** A daily floor of 8.33% of total liquidity-provision points is enforced so no single day dominates the week[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. If extreme volatility persists for ~20 hours of one day, that day's liquidity-provision points are capped at 50% of the weekly pool[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].

**LLP attribution / redistribution.** LLP is treated as a market maker under the same rules but its share is not allocated to LLP — remaining points are scaled so the total stays at 250,000[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers]. This effectively redistributes LLP-earned points to other platform participants[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].

**Partner attribution.** Third-party integrators (Partner Attribution Program) are a separate channel for fee revenue but are not directly tied to points payouts in the docs we have; integrators earn from user fees within Lighter's caps (perp 10 bps, spot 1%)[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-integrations-partner-attribution]. See [[entities/perpdex/lighter#mechanism]] for partner attribution detail.

## Eligibility and exclusions

- Points are earned by running organic trading strategies via UI and API[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program].
- Sybil, self-trading, and similar activities do not earn points[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program]. A Discord announcement on self-trading was issued to enforce a level playing field[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].
- Lighter applies multiple sybil-detection methods, both fully automated and semi-automated[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].
- Up to 10 accounts per user are allowed without penalty[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].
- Anti-farming and anti-sybil metrics are intentionally not disclosed to prevent gaming[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-retail].
- Only Premium accounts are eligible to earn market-making points[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers].
- Constants and parameters in the program may change at the team's discretion based on market conditions and data availability[^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program-market-makers][^lighter-docs-2026-04-28-points-program].
