# Aster

> A privacy-focused decentralized perpetual exchange spanning crypto, equity, and commodity markets, built on its own L1 (Aster Chain) with encrypted order flow, multi-product surface (Perps / Shield Mode / 1001x / Spot), and a venue-owned LP (ALP) sitting opposite trader flow.

## Snapshot
- Market structure: order-book Perpetuals plus AMM-style Shield Mode, one-click 1001x perps, and order-book Spot[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-what-is-aster]
- Operator: Aster team / Aster Foundation; treasury fully locked at TGE and gated on governance approval[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics]
- Native token: [[entities/token/aster]] (drives 5% perp-fee discount, governance, and staking emissions)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-aster-chain-staking-how-staking-works]
- Network: [[entities/network/aster-chain]] (own L1, 100k+ TPS / 50ms block latency target, encrypted-order pipeline)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-what-is-aster]
- Multi-chain footprint: Treasury contracts on BNB Chain, Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum; Earn products and USDF on BNB Chain; ALP mintable on BNB Chain and Arbitrum[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-smart-contracts][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp]
- Listing surface includes equity-style perps (AAPL/TSLA/NVDA/AMZN/META/GOOG/MSFT) and pre-launch contracts that auto-convert to perps[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-pre-launch-contracts]

## Mechanism

**Product surface.** Aster is positioned as a privacy-focused decentralized perp DEX with four trading types — order-book Perpetuals (the core product), AMM-style Shield Mode with built-in privacy and higher leverage, 1001x one-click MEV-resistant on-chain perps, and order-book Spot — plus an Earn layer covering BNB liquid staking (asBNB), the yield-bearing stablecoin USDF, and additional asUSDF/asBTC/asCAKE wrappers[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-what-is-aster][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-smart-contracts]. The headline differentiator is encrypted order flow: every order is encrypted before reaching the chain and only decrypted at execution, hiding position size, entry, and liquidation level from on-chain observers[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-what-is-aster].

**Aster Chain.** The platform runs on its own L1, Aster Chain, targeting 100,000+ TPS and 50ms block latency, designed specifically for private perps trading[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-what-is-aster]. Notably, the audit page does not list any audit covering the Aster Chain L1 itself or the perp matching engine — only the Vault, Earn, asBNB / asCAKE / asUSDF, and USDF token+minting contracts have published audits, all dated within 2024 (Sept–Dec)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-audit-reports]. Aster Treasury contracts span BNB Chain, Ethereum, Solana, and Arbitrum, but the Earn-product and USDF contracts are listed only on BNB Chain, consistent with BNB Chain being the primary execution venue for those products[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-overview-smart-contracts].

**Fee schedule.** The dedicated fees page states USDT-perps charge 0% maker / 0.04% taker and USD1-perps charge 0% maker / 0.005% taker (an order of magnitude cheaper on the taker side), computed on nominal value (contracts × transaction price)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees]. The Perpetuals overview page advertises a different rate (0.01% maker / 0.035% taker), creating a documented contradiction between marketing copy and the rate-card page[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals][^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees]. Depositing $ASTER into the perp wallet (or transferring from spot) yields a flat 5% fee discount that auto-applies — the canonical tokenomics ↔ fee linkage[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees]. Aster does not charge or receive funding; funding is peer-to-peer between longs and shorts[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-funding-rate].

**Mark price and oracle.** Mark price is the median of three components: Price 1 (Index × (1 + Funding × time-to-next-funding/8)), Price 2 (Index + 5-minute moving average of (Bid1+Ask1)/2 minus Index), and Contract Price[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-mark-price]. The Price Index is a volume-weighted average from major spot exchanges including Binance, HTX, Kraken, and Huobi[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-mark-price]. Defensive measures: any exchange deviating >5% from the median has its weight set to zero; if multiple exchanges show large deviations, the median replaces the weighted average; feeds lagging >3 seconds are excluded; if the contract trade strays >5% from mark for >5 seconds, contract price is replaced by mark in calculations[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-mark-price].

**Funding rate.** Aster uses a Binance-style formula F = [Average Premium Index P + clamp(interest_rate − P, −0.05%, 0.05%)] / (8/N), where the default interest rate component is 0.01% (BNBUSDT is an exception at 0%), N is the funding interval in hours, and the default interval is 8h with some pairs running shorter (ASTERUSDT at 4h)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-funding-rate]. The premium index is sampled every 5 seconds using impact-bid/ask prices computed against an Impact Margin Notional sized to the contract's max-leverage initial margin[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-funding-rate]. There is a 15-second timing deviation between actual time and when funding is charged, and Aster reserves discretion to adjust caps/floors/intervals during extreme volatility[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-funding-rate].

**Liquidations and ADL.** Aster uses Cross Margin by default; users can opt into Isolated Margin, but margin mode cannot be changed once a position or open order has been submitted[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-margin]. Maintenance margin is tier-based on total position size, not leverage selection[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-margin]. The "smart liquidation" waterfall has five steps: (1) cancel open orders, (2) issue one large IOC to partially close, (3) recheck margin and stop if maintenance is restored, (4) transfer any remaining bankrupt portion to the Insurance Fund at the bankruptcy price, (5) if the IF cannot fully cover the loss, fall through to ADL[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-liquidations]. Negative balances ≤5,000 USDT in USDT-perp accounts may be auto-covered by the insurance fund only when the account has no open positions and no offsetting transfers post-liquidation[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-liquidations]. ADL ranks counterparties by Leverage PnL quantile = (PnL percent × Margin ratio) where PnL percent = max(0, Unrealized profit) / max(1, Wallet balance); the most profitable + most highly leveraged opposing positions are reduced first, without warning[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-adl]. See [[concepts/risk/liquidation]] and [[concepts/risk/adl-waterfall]].

**Order types.** The order book supports Market, Limit, Stop Limit, Stop Market, Trailing Stop, and Post Only primitives plus TP/SL modifiers (referencing Mark or Last Price), Reduce Only, and BBO with Counterparty/Queue variants surfacing best-price vs fifth-best-price maker placement[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-order-types]. Time-in-force options are GTC, IOC, and FOK[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-order-types].

**Market Maker program.** Three preferential tiers gate on rolling 14-day volume — MM1 (≥$150M or 0.25% maker share, taker 2.3 bps / maker −0.25 bps), MM2 (≥$300M or 0.5%, 2.0 / −0.35), MM3 (≥$600M or 1%, 1.6 / −0.5)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program]. To count, a pair must meet a strict quoting standard for ≥70% of the UTC day: bid/offer sizes ≥ minimum, spread ≤ pair-specific cap (0.10% for BTC/ETH/SOL; 0.25% for most other crypto perps; 1.00% for equity perps like AAPL/TSLA/NVDA), with orders live ≥0.5 seconds[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program]. Maker rebates display as 0 in the UI but are calculated and disbursed hourly; transactions are visible in history[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program]. Application requires ≥$100M monthly futures volume on any platform plus IP whitelisting; the Aster team contacts applicants within three working days; Aster reserves unilateral right to modify, suspend, or terminate the program and disclaims any binding agreement[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-market-maker-program].

**Pre-launch contracts.** Aster lists tokens before they trade on major exchanges as expiry futures (sometimes perpetuals) that auto-convert to a regular perp once the underlying token launches[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-pre-launch-contracts]. The platform claims unilateral authority to initiate the conversion/delivery process either when the token launches externally or when tokenomics are released, and it reserves the right to delist contracts and to set the final delivery price[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-pre-launch-contracts]. In pre-market mode, Price 1 and Price 2 are anchored to the local pre-market order book's (Bid1+Ask1)/2 (no external Price Index); after conversion to perpetual, the standard perp index/mark methodology applies[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-pre-launch-contracts].

**ALP (venue-owned LP).** ALP is the platform's LP token: holders mint ALP to act as the market-maker counterparty for perp trades across all pairs, in the same architectural pattern as Hyperliquid's HLP and GMX's GLP[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp]. ALP's NAV moves with the pool's overall P&L — token price can rise or fall with trading activity and market conditions, so LPs bear directional/adverse-selection risk[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp]. Five yield sources accrue directly to NAV: ALP's share of market-making P&L from Simple Mode, trading fees, funding fees, liquidation captures, and platform-level fee collection[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp]. ALP can be minted on BNB Chain and Arbitrum with chain-specific deposit assets, cross-chaining the LP risk surface[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp]. Mint and burn fees adjust dynamically toward target weights — base parameters are FeeBasisPoints 0.25% and TaxBasisPoints 0.05% (USDT), and the rebalancing formulae are MintFee/BurnFee = max(FeeBasisPoints − TaxBasisPoints × InitialDiff/TargetValue, 0) when moving toward target, else FeeBasisPoints + TaxBasisPoints × min((InitialDiff+AfterDiff)/2, TargetValue)/TargetValue[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp-fees]. The published example, however, uses a TaxBasisPoints of 0.45% (not the listed 0.05%) and shows a 0% mint fee for adding underweight BTC versus a 0.7% burn fee for removing scarce BTC — a 70bps round-trip asymmetry — flagging either per-asset tax parameters or stale/inconsistent documentation[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp-fees].

**Tokenomics and revenue distribution.** $ASTER total supply is 8B, distributed Airdrop 53.5% / Ecosystem & Community 30% / Treasury 7% / Team 5% / Liquidity & Listings 4.5%; 8.8% (704M) unlocked at TGE for Aster Spectra and Aster Gems participants[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics]. The Airdrop allocation releases gradually over ~80 months (~7 years); the Team allocation has a 12-month cliff plus 40 months of linear vesting at 10M $ASTER per month[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics]. The Ecosystem & Community linear vesting was active for only 4 months (Oct 2025–Jan 2026) before being replaced by a staking emission model — staking rewards are now the sole active use of that bucket[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics]. Treasury (7%) is fully locked at TGE and only unlocks via governance approval[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics]. A portion of protocol revenue is redirected to $ASTER buybacks via the Aster Foundation and to governance rewards[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdaster-tokenomics].

**Staking.** Aster Chain emits 450,000 $ASTER per weekly Epoch at launch — 150K Base + 300K Loyalty[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-aster-chain-staking-how-staking-works]. Base APY is distributed proportionally across validators by share of network transactions processed; users earn pro-rata within a validator after validator commission[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-aster-chain-staking-how-staking-works]. Loyalty Rewards reward locked positions: User Power = veASTER × Trading Volume Boost, where veASTER = Locked Amount × (Remaining Lock / 208 weeks max) and the Trading Volume Boost is tiered (>$500K → 1.05×, >$50M → 1.15×, >$200M → 1.25×) — explicitly tying staking yield to personal trading volume, a structural governance ↔ usage link[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-aster-chain-staking-how-staking-works]. Epochs run Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 00:00 UTC with snapshot at Monday 00:00 UTC; deposits, top-ups, lock extensions, and redeem requests all take effect in the next Epoch (no immediate effect)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-aster-chain-staking-how-staking-works].

**Referral program.** The default rate is 10% commission on referee trading fees, configurably split between referrer and referee (e.g., 5%/5% as a fee discount); high-volume affiliates can apply for upgraded rates of 20% from VIP1 referees and 10% from VIP2+[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-referral]. Rebates settle in real time and credit by the next day; rates are calculated daily at 00:00 UTC and updated at 09:00 UTC; rewards are paid in the same asset the referee used to pay fees[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-referral]. Referral relationships are valid for 365 days by default with discretionary extension by Aster[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-referral]. Liquidation fees, self-trade volume, and zero-fee trade volume are excluded from commission calculations; self-referrals trigger full reward revocation; Aster reserves unilateral right to modify rates, rules, or cancel the programme[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-referral].

**Trade & Earn.** This program pays additional yield for using yield-bearing assets (USDF, asBNB) as perp trading collateral via Multi-Asset Mode (USDF 99.99% collateral ratio, asBNB 95%)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn]. Eligibility requires being active ≥2 days/week with weekly volume ≥50,000 USD (raised on 20 Nov 2025), and USDF holdings counted toward rewards are capped at 100,000 USDF per account (cap effective 4 Sept 2025)[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn]. Reward weeks run Thursday → Wednesday with payouts in USDF within 7 business days[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn]. Wash trading, market manipulation, and bulk-account farming trigger disqualification — the volume-threshold + reward-cap pairing structurally creates wash incentive that is acknowledged via these exclusion rules[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-program-trade-and-earn].

**USDF peg defense.** USDF maintains its 1:1 peg through three named mechanisms: rigid 1:1 redemption against USDT, market arbitrage on liquidity-pool deviations by external arbitrageurs, and delta-neutral hedging of underlying assets to suppress volatility transmission and generate fee yield[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdf-maintaining-the-peg]. The redemption guarantee depends on Aster maintaining an equal underlying balance available for redemption[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdf-maintaining-the-peg]. The page does not disclose a redemption fee, redemption SLA / queue / max daily capacity, the venue or counterparty of the delta-neutral hedge, or how the short leg is liquidated in stress — material gaps in peg-defense surface[^aster-docs-2026-04-28-usdf-maintaining-the-peg].

## Disputed claims
- **#dispute-fee-rates**: The Perpetuals overview page advertises 0.01% maker / 0.035% taker (`aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals#c4-c5`), while the dedicated Fees page states 0% maker / 0.04% taker for USDT-perps and 0% / 0.005% for USD1-perps (`aster-docs-2026-04-28-trading-perpetuals-fees#c1-c4`). The Fees page TL;DR self-flags the contradiction, suggesting the overview is marketing copy and the Fees page is the actual rate card — but neither page is marked authoritative.
- **#dispute-alp-tax-basis-points**: ALP Fees page lists TaxBasisPoints (USDT) at 0.05% (`aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp-fees#c5`), but the worked BTC example uses TaxBasisPoints 0.45% (`aster-docs-2026-04-28-earn-aster-alp-fees#c14`). Either per-asset tax parameters exist (BTC = 0.45%) and the page omits the table, or one of the values is stale.
